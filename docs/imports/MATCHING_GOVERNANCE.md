# Matching Governance — Rules, Thresholds & Override Policy

## Purpose

This document defines the governance model for semantic matching decisions in LiciGov Pro.
All matching decisions are subject to audit requirements under Lei 14.133/2021.

## Confidence Thresholds

| Level | Score Range | Policy |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| High | ≥ 0.85 | Auto-acceptance allowed; human review optional |
| Medium | 0.60 – 0.84 | Human review recommended |
| Low | 0.35 – 0.59 | Human review mandatory before acceptance |
| Uncertain | < 0.35 | Item flagged; mandatory re-search or manual entry |

## Consensus Score Policy

The consensus score is the weighted blend of four components:
- Lexical (30%), Semantic (35%), Normalization (20%), Parser (15%)

When `consensusScore < 0.85`, the item enters `review_pending` state automatically.

## Override Policy

Any human override requires:
1. A justification string (minimum 5 characters, not just whitespace)
2. Recording via `ReviewContract.addDecision()` with operation `override_candidate`
3. The override is immutable once added to the contract

Overrides are tracked in `review_decisions` table with full actor attribution.

## Escalation Policy

When a reviewer cannot reach a decision, they may use `escalateReview()` to forward to a
higher-level reviewer (identified by `escalateTo: userId`). Escalated items must be
re-reviewed within the session before finalization.

## Multi-Tenancy Rule

All matching operations require `organizationId`. Cross-organization data access is
prohibited. The `globalSemanticIndex` enforces org isolation via `getByOrg(organizationId)`.

## Determinism Requirement

All matching algorithms must be deterministic for replay purposes:
- Same `rawDescription` + `canonicalUnit` + `parserType` → same `replayKey`
- Consensus winner selection uses a 3-level tiebreak to guarantee uniqueness
- Non-deterministic fields (`createdAt`, `correlationId`) do NOT affect scoring

## Rejection Governance

Rejected candidates retain their rejection reason in the consensus aggregate.
Reasons are generated by `getRejectionReason()` and are human-readable.
Rejection does not delete the candidate — it is preserved for audit trail.

## Governance Alerts

The semantic drift service monitors for:
- `confidence_degradation`: avg confidence drop > 10% (warning) or > 20% (critical)
- `normalization_anomaly`: unit norm rate drop > 15% (warning)
- `semantic_volatility`: stddev > 0.20 (warning) or > 0.35 (critical)
- `ranking_inconsistency`: > 10% items with score < 0.50 (warning)
